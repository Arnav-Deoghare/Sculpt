# Code of Conduct

Sculpt is a project about personal progress, and many people who use it care about how they look. Treat everyone here with respect.

## What we expect

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with people new to open source or to Android.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and kind.
- Accept that people have different experience, bodies and goals. Don't comment on anyone's appearance, weight or fitness choices.
- Assume good intent, and ask when something is unclear.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Posting other people's photos or personal information without their consent. Please don't attach progress photos of yourself or anyone else to issues either.
- Spam, or promoting unrelated products.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may ban people who repeatedly do. To report a problem, open a private security advisory on the repository's **Security** tab and say it's a conduct report. Reports are handled privately.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone represents the project.
