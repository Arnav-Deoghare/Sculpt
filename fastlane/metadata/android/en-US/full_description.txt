Sculpt is a private photo logbook for tracking your physique over time. Everything stays on your phone.

<b>Privacy</b>
• Only the camera permission, and no internet permission at all
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics
• Photos live in the app's private storage, not your gallery

<b>Take consistent photos</b>
• Front, Front bicep, Side, Back and Back flexed poses, plus your own custom poses
• A ghost of your last photo to line yourself up
• Self-timer, flash, front and back cameras, and hands-free capture that shoots every pose in turn

<b>See your progress</b>
• Gallery grouped by day
• Compare your oldest and newest photo of a pose, or pick any two dates
• Side by side, or fade one photo over the other
• Pinch-zoom both photos together to check the same spot

Dark and light themes inspired by interlocking gym-mat tiles.

Sculpt is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0.
